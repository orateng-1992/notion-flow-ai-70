# Work Flow Wizard

AI-Powered Workplace Productivity Assistant
1. Project Objective

The objective of this project is to enhance workplace productivity for small business owners by automating common administrative and workplace tasks. The AI-powered assistant helps reduce the time spent on repetitive activities such as writing emails, summarising meetings, organising tasks, and planning daily work.

The project aims to help small business owners:

Save time on routine administrative tasks.
Improve communication through professionally written emails.
Quickly convert meeting discussions into useful summaries and action items.
Organise and prioritise daily tasks.
Improve productivity and reduce manual administrative work.
2. Tools Used
ChatGPT

Used for:

Drafting and improving professional emails.
Summarising meeting notes.
Creating task lists and action plans.
Prioritising tasks.
Generating professional workplace communication.
Creating templates for repetitive administrative activities.
Google Gemini

Used for:

Generating alternative ideas and responses.
Summarising information.
Brainstorming workplace solutions.
Comparing different approaches to completing tasks.
Supporting research and content generation.
Notion

Used as the central workspace for organising and managing information, including:

Tasks and to-do lists.
Meeting notes.
Action items.
Project planning.
Deadlines and priorities.
Business information and documentation.
3. Core Features & Prompts Used
A. Email Automation

Feature:
The assistant helps small business owners create professional emails quickly based on short instructions or rough notes.

Prompt used:

"Act as a professional workplace communication assistant. Convert my notes into a clear, professional and concise email. Use a friendly but professional tone and include a suitable subject line and clear call to action."

Example use:
A business owner provides a few points about a customer enquiry, and the AI turns them into a complete professional response.

B. Meeting Summary Automation

Feature:
The assistant converts meeting notes or transcripts into structured summaries.

Prompt used:

"Summarise the following meeting notes. Identify the key discussion points, decisions made, action items, responsible persons and deadlines. Present the information in a clear and easy-to-read format."

Output includes:

Meeting overview
Key discussion points
Decisions made
Action items
Responsible person
Deadlines
C. Task Planning & Prioritisation

Feature:
The assistant creates an organised daily or weekly task plan and prioritises tasks according to urgency and importance.

Prompt used:

"Create a daily task plan from the following list of tasks. Prioritise the tasks according to urgency and importance. Include suggested time slots and identify tasks that can be delegated or scheduled for later."

Example output:

Priority	Task	Suggested Time
High	Respond to urgent customer emails	08:30
High	Prepare client quotation	10:00
Medium	Update business records	13:00
Low	Organise digital files	15:00
D. Meeting-to-Task Conversion

Feature:
The assistant turns meeting discussions into actionable tasks.

Prompt used:

"Review these meeting notes and identify all tasks that need to be completed. For each task, provide the task description, responsible person, priority and deadline."

This allows information from meetings to be converted into a practical task list that can be managed in Notion.
